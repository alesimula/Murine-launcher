r4l.launcher is Rafael Maciel's personal Android launcher, built on Murine Launcher and AOSP Launcher3.

Organize your app drawer with optional offline categories. Create and rename groups, choose their icons, swipe between categories, and move apps by hand. Sorting stays on your device.

Move app search to the bottom of the drawer, above the keyboard. Both category tabs and bottom search are off by default and can be enabled in Launcher settings.

Customize your home screen with grids, icon packs, widgets, gestures, and backups inherited from Murine. Supports Android 8 and above. Some effects depend on Android version and device support.
